# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, safe, and inclusive project environment for
contributors and users, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical and operational risk directly.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Protect private imagery, credentials, location data, and customer or research
  information.
- Keep discussions focused on the litter-detection use case and the safety of
  people affected by its deployment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
information, sexualized conduct, and sustained personal attacks are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents or sensitive evidence in a public issue. Contact
the repository owner privately through their verified GitHub profile. Security
vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or access when behavior
violates this policy. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
